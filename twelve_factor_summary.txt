Tutorial 5 Summary

The Movie Microservice can follow the Twelve-Factor methodology by using:
- one codebase
- explicit dependencies
- external configuration
- replaceable backing services
- separated build/release/run stages
- stateless processes
- port binding
- horizontal concurrency
- disposability
- dev/prod parity
- centralized logs
- one-off admin processes

Result:
The system becomes easier to deploy, scale, maintain and operate in a cloud environment.
